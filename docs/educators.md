# Educators & academic access

OpenDubs is built to be **replicated in classrooms and research labs** — so much of the
toolchain and hardware it depends on is available **free or discounted for educational use.**
This project itself is a **beneficiary of the NVIDIA Academic Grant Program.** If you're an
educator, student, or academic researcher, start with the programs below before you buy or
subscribe to anything.

!!! note "These are third-party programs"
    Each program is run by its provider (Autodesk, Figma, NVIDIA, goBILDA/ServoCity), and
    **eligibility, discounts, and terms are set by them and change over time.** The figures
    here are current at the time of writing — always confirm on the program's own page.

## Design & CAD software — free for education

The mechanical CAD and the diagrams in this guide are both made with tools that are **free for
students and educators.**

- **[Autodesk Fusion — Education](https://www.autodesk.com/education/edu-software/fusion)** —
  the CAD used for the OpenDubs chassis and every embedded 3D viewer in this guide. Free
  educational licenses for students and educators; the [Fusion links throughout the assembly
  guide](build/lower-frame.md) open in the free viewer regardless.
- **[Figma — Education](https://www.figma.com/education/)** — the tool behind the wiring,
  bus, and network diagrams. Free Education plan for students and educators.

## Compute — NVIDIA academic programs

The autonomy brain is an **NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor.**

- **[NVIDIA Academic Grant Program](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/industries/higher-education-research/academic-grant-program/)**
  — supports university research with compute and hardware. **OpenDubs is a beneficiary of this
  program.** If you're at a university, it's worth applying before purchasing Jetson hardware.

## Hardware — goBILDA / ServoCity educational discounts

The chassis is built from **[goBILDA](https://www.gobilda.com) structural parts (sold through
[ServoCity](https://www.servocity.com))**, both of which run educational-discount programs for
schools, universities, homeschools, and robotics competitions.

- **[goBILDA — Education](https://www.gobilda.com/education)** — educational discounts (and an
  FTC competition discount) for qualifying institutions and programs.
- **[ServoCity — Educational Discounts](https://www.servocity.com/educational-discounts)** — a
  standing educational discount for qualifying institutions.

Applying takes an institutional ID and a short form; once approved, the discount is applied to
your account. Discounts typically **exclude tax and shipping** — check each page for current
terms. ServoCity also runs [educational sponsorships](https://www.servocity.com/educational-sponsorships)
and [scholarships](https://www.servocity.com/scholarships) worth a look for student teams.

## Reduce, reuse, recycle

Much of what this robot is built from — goBILDA structural parts, motors, hubs, wheels, and the
fastener sets — is the same stock that **FIRST** teams use, both FTC and FRC. If you finish a build
with parts left over, or retire a robot, consider donating them to a local team rather than letting
them sit in a drawer. Equally, a nearby team's surplus bin is a good first place to look before
ordering.
